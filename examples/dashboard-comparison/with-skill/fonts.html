Pick the dashboard font

Each card sets the same slice of the Selat Line dashboard in one font, at the sizes and weights the dashboard uses (12, 14, 16 and 28px; 400 and 500). Every font here is free for commercial use, with no trial limits. Cards are grouped by the style each one stands in for. Green tags are from the font-picks list, amber tags are fonts font-picks calls AI-looking, grey tags are free look-alikes. Open any card on the full dashboard to judge it in place.

Paid originals (ABC Diatype, TWK Lausanne, Saans, PP Mori, Aeonik, GT Walsheim, Circular, ABC Favorit, Monument Grotesk, Söhne Mono, GT America Mono) have no legal free version, so they are not shown. "Stands in for" is a judgment of look and feel, not a claim by the font's author. Departure Mono and Server Mono have one weight only, which is all the mono text in the dashboard needs.
